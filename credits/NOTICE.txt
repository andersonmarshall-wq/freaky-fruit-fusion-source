# Sources and credits

- Base game: **asetsiya / Wateru**, https://github.com/asetsiya/wateru — MIT license retained in `LICENSE`.
- Engine: **Defold**, https://defold.com. Camera dependency: **britzl / defold-orthographic 3.6.3**, https://github.com/britzl/defold-orthographic.
- Fruit artwork: **RDBI / Kawaii Fruits**, https://rdbi.itch.io/kawaii-fruits — supplied by Marshall after purchase. Honeydew, cantaloupe, whole golden watermelon, and expression variants adapt these vectors.
- Music: **chajamakesmusic / cute & silly rpg music pack**, https://chajamakesmusic.itch.io/cute-and-silly-rpg-music-pack — **CC BY 4.0**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. Supplied archive: `poopie pack.zip`. Selected tracks: `blossom.wav` and `regrowth wip.wav`. Adaptations: slower tempo, low-pass filtering, volume normalization, OGG conversion, and in-game crossfades. No creator endorsement is implied.
- Soft effects: **Candle Light / Tiny Pops & Sparkles — 80 Free Game SFX**, https://candlelightgame.itch.io/tiny-pops-sparkles-80-free-game-sfx — the creator permits personal and commercial game use. Game-ready conversions use the selections below.
- Font: **Niskala Huruf / Mouldy Cheese**, https://www.fontspace.com/mouldy-cheese-font-f95405 — the author permits commercial projects. Obtain the TTF locally; it is not mirrored in the public source repository.
- Launcher icon: generated with OpenAI Image Generation for this project, then exported at Android launcher sizes; see `art/app-icon/PROMPT.md`.
- Elf gibberish and startled squeals: synthetic Flite `slt` utterances, pitch/tempo/loudness processed with FFmpeg; no human voice imitation. Uwu, shake, and game-over cues are original synthesized tones.

Purchased assets keep their own terms and are not relicensed by the upstream game's MIT license.
The fruit pack permits editing and commercial/non-commercial game use but prohibits redistribution of the asset files. Credit is appreciated but does not replace that restriction. Its raw and adapted fruit assets must not be exposed in a public source repository without the creator's permission.

The music's CC BY 4.0 license permits redistribution with attribution, the license link, and identification of changes. The sound pack's page permits game use but does not explicitly grant standalone asset redistribution. This public source release omits restricted fruit files and the six pack effects, including from its history, and links to the original download pages. See `ASSETS.md` for local setup.
